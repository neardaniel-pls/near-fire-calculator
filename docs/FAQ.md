# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### Is this financial advice?
No. This is an educational tool. Results are projections based on historical assumptions. Always consult a professional before making investment decisions.

### Does this work for non-Portuguese markets?
The calculator uses generic financial math. Adjust salary, inflation, and return assumptions for your market. Currency is display-only.

### Is my data saved?
All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. Clear your browser data to reset.

## Features

### How does Monte Carlo simulation work?
It generates thousands of random scenarios based on your portfolio's expected return and volatility. Each scenario follows a different path. The result shows the probability of reaching your FIRE goal.

### What are financial events?
One-time or recurring events that affect your savings:
- **One-time**: Inheritance, house down payment, etc.
- **Recurring**: Annual bonus, rental income, etc.

### Can I save my portfolio?
Yes. Use the "Save" button to store your portfolio as a template. It's saved in localStorage.

### How do I export results?
Click "Export PDF" to generate a PDF report with your projection chart and summary statistics.

## Troubleshooting

### The page looks broken
Try a modern browser (Firefox, Chrome, Safari). The calculator uses modern CSS and ES6 modules.

### Numbers look wrong
Check that your inputs are reasonable:
- Annual return: 4-10% is typical for diversified portfolios
- Volatility: 10-25% is typical
- Inflation: 2-3% is typical for the Eurozone
